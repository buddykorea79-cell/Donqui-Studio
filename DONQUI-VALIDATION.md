It creates synthetic image/video/audio sources and a fresh isolated profile under `.local/donqui-validation`, imports and trims music to 5 seconds, renders a 1.5-second photo effect, fits a 3.5-second video effect to the remainder, exports MP4 and reopens the project. Native file pickers are replaced only within this automated test. The script verifies project files and decodes the exported MP4, and saves screenshots plus stream metadata.

## Startup

# V.Clip across videos, Media composition, Ollama in AI settings — 2026-09-24

Asked for: work on many clips from many videos at once, as AutoVeo does; preview and adjust clips; a 5-second default clip length that can be changed; the Ollama options in the AI settings; V.Clip only splitting, with Media composing videos and photos under Remotion effects; and Media fitting clips and photos to the Audio tab's music or narration.

## What changed

- **V.Clip** lists every source video on one page, each with its segments (AutoVeo's 영상 클립 page). **모든 영상 구간 찾기** analyses the videos one after another through a single queue; each panel can also analyse, cancel or be left waiting on its own. The clip length (2–20 s, default 5, kept per machine) sets the analysis windows and the length of a segment added by hand; **모든 구간에 적용** gives every segment that length. A card previews its range from the source, looping, and sets its start or end from playback; start, end and length also take typed values and ±0.5 s nudges. Analysis never overwrites segments added by hand or split already.
- **Splitting only records ranges.** Selected segments go into the project's new optional `mediaBoard.clips` (additive, no schema bump) in one undoable write — 290 ms for five clips of 4K footage below, against ~75 s per clip rendered before. A segment changed after its split shows **바뀜 · 다시 나누기** and updates the same clip. V.Clip no longer shows the timeline strip: nothing it does lands there.
- **Media** has the material on the left — V.Clip's clips, photos (kept as their original files, several per pick) and whole videos (transcript drawer, V.Clip hand-off, direct timeline add) — and the **composition** on the right: an ordered list where each item carries its own length and Remotion effect (random resolved by a stored seed), with a live Remotion preview. Photos now take effects too: the composition renders them through `<Img>`, at any length; a photo with no effect is converted by FFmpeg as before. The main process re-approves the photos a project declares when it loads it, so they render after a restart.
- **Fit to audio**: items stretch or shrink together, in proportion, within their own bounds (a photo holds as long as asked; a clip grows into its source — end first, then start), in whole frames at 30 fps so the composition ends on the audio's last frame. Clips of one video grow only into footage no other clip of it shows: the gap between two is shared at its middle, and a neighbour that already overlaps an end stops growth there (V.Clip's own segments overlap by up to a second). Only when that unused footage cannot cover the audio do clips grow into each other's footage, and the fit warns that some scenes now play twice. When the narration has a transcript, each cut moves onto the nearest pause within 1 s that every item's bounds allow (AutoVeo's "말이 끊기는 곳").
- **타임라인에 적용** renders what needs rendering (cached by content), then places the whole composition in one undoable edit: replacing the timeline after a confirmation, or appending. Audio anchored to replaced clips is pinned to the programme time it played at rather than dropped. Progress survives tab switches and can be stopped. Renders already in the project are reused instead of listed twice.
- **AI settings** gain a *로컬 비전 모델 (Ollama)* section — status, model (lightest by default, or none), re-check — translated into all 15 locales. V.Clip shows the model in use and links there.
- **Ollama fix** found on real footage: Qwen3-VL on Ollama 0.34.4 ignores `think: false`, reasons until `num_predict` runs out and returns neither `content` nor `thinking` with the answer — every window of three DJI clips fell back to frame quality. The request now starts the assistant's reply with `{`, which the model continues directly: a full schema-shaped answer in ~1.3 s instead of 4 s of reasoning. Tags given as one comma string are accepted.

## Found along the way

- **Qwen3-VL answered nothing.** On Ollama 0.34.4 the model ignores `think: false`, spends all of `num_predict` reasoning (`done_reason: "length"`) and returns an empty `content` and a truncated `thinking` — every window fell back to frame quality. Measured on one window: request as before, 4 s and no answer; with the reply prefilled with `{`, 1.3 s and a full, schema-conforming answer. Fixed in `ollamaVision.ts` (see above).
- **A fit could show the same footage twice, without a word.** Every clip could grow anywhere in its source, so in the first `--no-ai` run the two clips of the 10.1 s video became 0–4.7 s and 0.07–10.1 s: the same 4.6 s played twice. There the footage could not cover 20 s any other way, but nothing kept clips apart when it could, and nothing said so when it could not. The fit now grows clips into unused footage first and warns when it has to reuse some (see above).
- **The synthetic smoke closed the preview it had opened.** It failed once at the composition's item preview: two clicks 2 s apart, and the preview closed at the end. Traced over 19 further runs (the renderer's input events and long tasks in all of them, the main process's event-loop lag in the last nine), the app opened the preview within about 300 ms of the click reaching it every time, mostly spent mounting the Remotion player, and the main process never lagged. In 3 of the 19, though, Playwright's CDP round trips took seconds: 3.5 s to acknowledge a click, 5–10 s to find the button again. The smoke's retry read that as a lost click after 2 s and clicked again, and the button toggles. It now looks at the button's state for up to 15 s before clicking again, and logs how long the preview took; a later run needed 7.2 s and passed. Why CDP stalls is not known, and it did not recur in six runs with the protocol logged; nothing a user does goes through CDP.
- **An effect choice could save as the old effect.** The composition's effect `<select>` read `event.target.value` inside the queued write, by which time React had put the controlled select back — caught by the Media component test, fixed by reading it in the handler.
- **The smoke's Electron was killed under Playwright's launcher.** With `_electron.launch`, Electron exited with code 1 at the first effect render in all three runs of the real-footage flow (and in one of three runs that only applied a saved composition). No exit event fired in the main process, no uncaught error or crash report was recorded, main-process memory was ~200 MB, and a process watcher saw no `taskkill` near the moment. Launched directly — spawned as `start-donqui.cmd` spawns it, with the window driven over CDP — the full flow passed three times (once with Playwright's two extra stdio pipes added back) and the apply once. The cause is not identified. Both smokes now launch Electron that way, through `scripts/electron-direct-launch.cjs`, which says why; `donqui-smoke.cjs` had passed under the launcher too.
